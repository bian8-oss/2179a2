Set Aside

Australia has protected an area larger than Germany, France and Spain combined.
This is the story of where, who, and whether it's enough.

By J. Bian · May 2026
01

The Big Picture

"Australia has protected an area larger than Germany, France and Spain combined." It's one of the world's great conservation experiments — but is it working?

Australia has protected more than 18.6 million square kilometres — an area larger than Germany, France and Spain combined. It's one of the most ambitious conservation experiments on Earth. But numbers can be deceiving...

02

The Paradox

"22% of the country is protected. But of Australia's 89 bioregions, 23 fall below the international 17% target." We've protected a lot — just not always the right places.

Australia leads the world in scale, but the quality of that protection is uneven. Many of our most biologically rich regions remain under-protected. And most of our protected area is in lower protection categories.

03

The Quiet Revolution

"In 1998, Australia did something no other country had done. It handed land back." Today, Indigenous Protected Areas make up nearly half of the National Reserve System.

It began on Anangu Country with the Nantawarrina IPA in 1998. Since then, Indigenous peoples have established more than 90 IPAs, covering over 85 million hectares — an area larger than France. This is partnership, recognition and Country healing.

Fig 6 — IPA growth ribbon timeline (cumulative area, 1997 → 2024)
Fig 7 — Choropleth: % of each state's protected area governed by Indigenous communities

IPAs cover >85 million hectares and are concentrated across the north and central Australia where Indigenous people have cared for Country for millennia.

04

A Century of Protection

"From a single park in 1879 to 14,000 today. The story of Australia's protected areas is the story of changing values."

From a single park in 1879 to more than 14,000 protected areas today. Each era reflects changing values — from colonial recreation to biodiversity conservation to Indigenous land management.

05

Victoria's Paradox

"Victoria is home to a quarter of all Australians — but only 4% of its area is protected." We live next to one of the country's biggest gaps.

High population. High biodiversity. High threat. Low protection. Look closely at the map of Victoria and a different story emerges — the state has been settled and farmed more thoroughly than any other. What remains is fragmented.

06

Looking Forward

"By 2030, every nation has pledged to protect 30% of its land and sea. Australia is closer than most. But the gap isn't measured in hectares — it's measured in which hectares."

We've protected an extraordinary amount. We've made history with Indigenous partnership. We've seen decades of progress. The next leap won't be about hectares. It will be about protecting the places that matter most — for nature, for culture and for the future of this country.
